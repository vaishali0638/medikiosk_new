## The three that are not written

**18, 19 and 22** are all hospital-facing documents rather than engineering artifacts, and
all three would be better written with the hospital than for it — a governance guide that
does not match a committee's actual constitution, or a pilot guide written without knowing
the department, is a document nobody follows. The underlying mechanisms are built and
documented in the phase notes.

## The one that matters most

**7.** Everything else on this list can be exercised. The seed content cannot be used on a
patient, because no practitioner has authored or reviewed it — and that is what blocks
Phase 17's pilot and item 12 of the readiness checklist. It is Phase 0 work, it was never
started, and no engineering substitutes for it.
